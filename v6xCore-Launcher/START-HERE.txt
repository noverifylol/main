v6xCore Launcher 3.0 / Mono Edition

1. Close any older v6xCore instance.
2. Put v6xCore Launcher.exe in its own folder, or extract the full ZIP.
3. Enter a customer license for Counter Strike 2. Do not enter the application secret.
4. After sign-in, select Launch on the CS2 card.
5. Use windowed/borderless CS2. Aim starts off and requires a held key.
6. FFA mode and Target teammates are under Aim control > Player relations.
7. Saved settings are now called Configs.

No key yet? Run preview-ui.cmd, then choose Preview library.
Preview mode cannot access CS2 or emit aim input.

Capture privacy is a best-effort Windows request, not a universal streamproof guarantee.
The other seven game cards are Coming soon, with artwork but no executable module.

Read README.md and BUILD_REPORT.md for setup, tests, and limitations.
No driver, injection, silent bullet redirection, forced-hit feature, or concealed payload.
